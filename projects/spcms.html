Business systems—Private business software

SPCMS.

Commission logic, made visible. A sales, referral, and commission management platform connected to Shopify operations.

PROJECTSPCMS
CATEGORYBusiness systems
STAGEDeployed · integration refinement
STACKPython · Flask · SQL · Shopify webhooks
MY INVOLVEMENTSystem architecture, backend, interfaces, integration and deployment

The challenge.

Tracking referred sales, employee commissions, and team performance across an active sales operation is easy to fragment across spreadsheets.

What I worked on

SPCMS brings sales attribution, team structures, commission rules, and payroll-related views into a single management workflow.

“Good software is measured by what it helps people do.”

Core workflows

  • Employee and team management with role-aware views
  • Individual tracking links for Shopify referral attribution
  • Revenue and commission calculations for employees and team leads
  • Payroll views, sales monitoring, and training resource support

Where it stands

A deployed application undergoing continued Shopify sale-attribution and commission verification.
